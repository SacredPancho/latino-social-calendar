Latino Social Calendar

Latino Social Calendar is a private, single-owner content-planning application built and used exclusively by its owner/operator to plan, research, and publish social media content for the Mexillicious social media channels (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook).

What this application does

Latino Social Calendar helps its owner: research trending Latino news and culture stories, organize them into a content calendar, generate cover images for planned posts, and publish finished videos directly to the owner's own YouTube channel (the Mexillicious Brand Account) using the YouTube Data API. The app also drafts suggested captions and hashtags for the owner to review before anything is posted.

Who uses this application

This is not a public product, service, or website. There is no public sign-up, no other user accounts, and no data collected from anyone other than the single owner/operator who built and runs it for their own use.
